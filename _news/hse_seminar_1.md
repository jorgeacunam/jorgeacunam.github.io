The **HSE Lab Seminar Series** kicked off at the UAI Viña del Mar campus with **[Prof. Joris van de Klundert](https://jorgeacunam.github.io/team.html)**, who presented *How good can your survival model be despite a low C Index?*. The series is a biweekly, hybrid brown-bag seminar where faculty, graduate students, and invited guests share their work in health systems engineering.
