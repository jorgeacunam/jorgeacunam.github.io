Researchers from the Health Systems Engineering Lab (HSE Lab) at **[Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez](https://www.uai.cl/)** presented two healthcare innovation projects at Hospital Dr. Gustavo Fricke focused on improving operational efficiency through artificial intelligence and predictive analytics. The initiatives included SOPHIA, a system for operating room optimization, and a Fast Track prediction model for emergency departments. Authorities and representatives from both UAI and Hospital Dr. Gustavo Fricke participated in the meeting, highlighting the importance of collaboration between academia and public healthcare institutions. Photos and further information can be found [here](https://www.facebook.com/share/1XexqtRe6H/).
