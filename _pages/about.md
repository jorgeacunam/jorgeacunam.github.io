Jorge Acuña is an Assistant Professor of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research and the founder and director of the [Health Systems Engineering Laboratory (HSE Lab)](https://jorgeacunam.github.io/HSE/) at Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez (UAI), where he also serves as Head of the Master of Engineering Sciences (MCI) program, Industrial Engineering specialization. He also holds a Courtesy Professor position in Industrial and Management Systems Engineering at the University of South Florida [(USF)](https://www.usf.edu/engineering/imse/). His research is built upon the intersection of operations research and healthcare systems to improve patient outcomes and access. 

Professor Acuña has a track record of developing innovative mathematical models anchored in optimization, game theory, and stochastic programming to address critical health issues. Current research activities focus on elective surgery waiting lists, surgical scheduling, cancer care analytics, home healthcare planning, kidney transplant allocation, emergency department overcrowding, length of stay prediction, hospital food waste, and emergency medical services. His work is funded by ANID through a FONDECYT Initiation Grant (2026-2029). 

He received his Ph.D. (2021) and MSc. (2018) in Industrial Engineering from USF and a B.S. in Civil Industrial Engineering from Universidad de La Frontera in 2015. He teaches optimization and health decision-making at undergraduate and graduate levels, and has mentored doctoral and master's students at UAI and USF.
