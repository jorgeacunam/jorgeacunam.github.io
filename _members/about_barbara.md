    Bárbara Güichaquelén Leviñanco received her Master in Engineering Sciences (Industrial Engineering) from Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez in December 2025, under the supervision of Dr. Jorge Acuña. Her thesis, "Learning Models for Estimating Operating Room Times in Orthopedic and Trauma Surgery," focused on estimating surgical times in the traumatology and orthopaedic area of a hospital in Chile. The main objective was to obtain a better estimate of the time of surgeries to facilitate planning, better use of resources and reduce postponements in operating rooms.
